## Full description

Rain falls on a neon city. The Enforcers hold the streets; the Neon Gangs want them back. Who wins is up to you.

Showdown: Future City Battle is a two-player strategy board game with blocky toy pieces on a glowing circuit-board city.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Each side gets 2 actions a turn: move one square along a road, or shoot
• Every piece has its own range: squads, heroes, patrol cars, turrets, bikes and hoverboards
• Shots hit 2 times in 3. Heroes can land a special hit that takes a piece out in one go
• Win by wiping out the other side, or by reaching their base

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Pass the phone to a friend, or play against the robot
• Let the robot play both sides and watch
• Six maps

A CITY THAT FEELS ALIVE
• Police cars and gang vans scream overhead, and an advert blimp drifts by
• Lock down! and System hacked! special attacks shake the whole screen
• Rain, sirens, trains, radio chatter and buzzing neon, all made in code

No ads. No in-app purchases. No data collected. Works offline.
